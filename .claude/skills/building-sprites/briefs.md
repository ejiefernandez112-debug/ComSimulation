# Design briefs for the buildings still without art

Read only the entries you're working on. Each: id · size · what it should show (the signature
prop in **bold**) · ideas for variants B and C. A building that can make several products (it picks
one for good when built) gets one picture for all of them: show a mix, or something general.
Which batches are done is in plan.md §4 and on the encyclopedia page; this file isn't updated per batch.

Done before variants existed (one design each): Plantation (`wheat_farm`), Bakery, Grain Mill
(`flour_mill`), City Hall, Construction Office, Public Housing, Warehouse, Wind Turbine, Electric
Substation, Solar and Nuclear Power Plants.

## Batch B: homes, utilities, shops
- `makeshift_hut` · 1x1 · a shack put up by homeless families: patched **tarp roof**, crates,
  campfire, washing line. B: corrugated-metal shack. C: lean-to tent against a pallet wall. Keep it
  small and humble; it's the only building without a ground plot.
- `small_house` (Regular House) · 1x1 · cottage with a **pitched roof and a garden fence**, mailbox,
  flower beds. B: other wall and roof colours, a bike. C: a narrow two-storey townhouse.
- `villa` · 1x1 · rich home: two storeys, big windows, **swimming pool** (water), palm or round
  trees, sun loungers. B: white and terracotta, Mediterranean. C: modern flat roof with glass.
- `water_treatment_plant` · 2x2 · **round settling tanks full of water**, pipes, a pump house, a
  blue-white water sign. B: other colours, a water tower. C: long rectangular basins.
- `supermarket` · 2x2 · shop with a **glass front and a big sign**, trolleys, parking spaces,
  fruit crates outside. B: other brand colours. C: warehouse-style store with a long awning.
- `trading_post` · 2x2 · trader from off the island: **market stall with crates and barrels**, a
  flag, a little jetty or anchor (harbour feel). B: other colours. C: a striped bazaar tent.

## Batch C: farming
- `feed_mill` · 2x2 · makes Animal Feed from corn or soy meal: **hopper and silo** with a
  conveyor, feed sacks. B: colours. C: two smaller silos and a shed.
- `ranch` · 2x2 · cattle, pigs, chickens, milk or eggs: stable, fenced pen with **a cow and a few
  hens**, hay. B: colours, a pig instead of a hen. C: round-roof barn, bigger pasture.
- `fishery` · 2x2 · fish or shrimp: a small pond or water inlet with a **jetty and a fishing
  boat**, nets drying, crates of fish. B: colours. C: shrimp ponds in squares.
- `apiary` · 2x2 · honey and beeswax: rows of **bee boxes (hives)**, flower beds, a small honey
  house with a jar sign. B: colours. C: a round straw-skep style.
- `sugar_mill` · 2x2 · sugar from sugarcane: crusher shed with a **chimney**, **stacks of cane**,
  sugar sacks. B: colours. C: tall boiler house.

## Batch D: food factories 1
Factories may share one "factory hall" layout, but each gets its own roof colour and signature prop.
- `oil_press` · 2x2 · cooking oil and soy meal: **round oil tanks**, a press house, bottle crates.
- `food_factory` · 2x2 · pasta, cereal, rice, chips, packaged food: conveyor hall, **stacks of
  boxes**, a loading truck.
- `confectionery` · 2x2 · candy and chocolate: pink and cream trim, a **giant candy or lollipop sign**.
- `beverage_plant` · 2x2 · juice, soft drinks, coffee: **tall silver tanks**, crates of bottles.
- `cannery` · 2x2 · canned fruit and fish: **pyramid of cans**, a conveyor, a can-shaped sign.

## Batch E: food factories 2
- `dairy` · 2x2 · cheese, butter, yogurt: white barn-factory, **milk tank**, a cow or cheese sign.
- `slaughterhouse` · 2x2 · beef, hide, pork, chicken: a plain hall with a **loading bay and a
  livestock truck**. Keep it clean and friendly: no gore.
- `meat_plant` · 2x2 · burgers, sausages, nuggets: cold-storage block with **roof vents**, a
  refrigerated truck, a sausage or burger sign.
- `fish_plant` · 2x2 · frozen fish, packaged seafood: dockside hall, **ice boxes**, a fish sign.
